Selected five original glTF models, their buffers and referenced textures are preserved here.
Runtime copies are normalized in metres, textures limited to 512 pixels and geometry packed
by tools/regions/prepare-visual-assets.py. Grass, flowers and fern are used only in the regional scene.
The archive and engine projects are not shipped. No paid edition or original engine shader was used.
